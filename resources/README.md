# Fovium resources policy

Role: Contract for tracked Fovium resources and the ignored local imaging corpus.
Read when: Adding an image, icon, font, generated asset, or test image corpus.
Authoritative for: What may be committed beneath `resources/`, provenance requirements, and local-corpus boundaries.
Not authoritative for: Runtime resource loading, application packaging, or dependency versions.

## Tracked shipped and verification resources

Curated assets intended to ship with or verify Fovium live under `resources/`. Current tracked examples include project-authored localization catalogs and R8-A's attributed MIT-licensed derived color-name catalog under `color-names/`; future branding or test fixtures require normal source-control review. Every externally sourced or adapted tracked asset must have its source, author, license, and use recorded in [`docs/THIRD-PARTY.md`](../docs/THIRD-PARTY.md) before commit.

Do not commit unverified internet images or raw generation dumps. Tracked test image corpora must have known provenance and compatible terms or be generated by the project with that method recorded. User photographs and runtime data must never become source-controlled resources.

## Ignored local engineering corpus

`resources/test-images/` is the explicit exception only in physical location: it may contain developer-local photographs and mutable imaging specimens under `resources/`, but the whole tree is ignored and is never a source-controlled resource. It is not shipped, is not repository provenance, and must not be copied into tracked resource paths.

For relevant imaging, rendering, navigation, metadata, codec, or performance work, Codex may inspect and reorganize this local tree and create, copy, rename, corrupt, or remove derived specimens inside it. Prefer mutating a copy when an original is useful. Automated tests and tracked documentation must not depend on its contents or publish its inventory.
